\section*{Headline subsets}

This supplement lists the twelve headline subsets of the main text: for each dataset--backbone case, the features selected by the frozen full-DEV refit of the LLM-assisted family leader and of the classical family leader, in the order the selector produced them ($\Kfeat=20$ for logistic regression, $\Kfeat=40$ for CatBoost). Each dataset takes two tables, the first giving ranks 1 to 20 of all four subsets and the second ranks 21 to 40 of the two CatBoost subsets: Tables~\ref{tab:subsets_homecredit} and \ref{tab:subsets_homecredit_b} for Home Credit, Tables~\ref{tab:subsets_lendingclub} and \ref{tab:subsets_lendingclub_b} for LendingClub v2, and Tables~\ref{tab:subsets_stability} and \ref{tab:subsets_stability_b} for Stability 2024. For pure LLM the order is that of the cached full-DEV ranking; on Stability 2024 one ranking served both backbones, so its logistic-regression subset is the first twenty names of its CatBoost subset. Feature names are those of the engineered universes described in the main text; on Home Credit the three external scores occupy the first three ranks of the pure LLM list. The lists are also provided as \texttt{headline\_subsets.csv} in the repository.
